# Sample photos

The demo uses real photos, not generated ones: openly licensed photos from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), taken by their authors in Salt Lake (Kolkata) in 2017–2018 and in Hingalganj (Sundarbans) in 2025, with the camera's GPS and capture time intact.

**The organisation (Green Delta Collective), its two projects, the field officers and every note are fictional.** The photos were not taken for this project, and nothing here claims that the people or places in them are connected with it.

* `python scripts/fetch_sample_photos.py` downloads the originals into `demo_photos/commons/` and checks each against the SHA-1 that Commons publishes. The list is in [`scripts/sample_photos.json`](../scripts/sample_photos.json).
* `python scripts/seed_demo.py` sends them to the two field devices with the notes and status reports from that list.

## What the apps do with them

Saakshi resizes the photos, blurs faces, draws before/after composites, crops campaign images and writes captions. Those are adaptations: they carry the same credit and, for the CC BY-SA photos, the same licence.

* The "before/after" at the Sector V courtyard pairs the storm-damage photo (16 May 2017) with photos of the same premises being washed (14 Aug 2017). They show different parts of the premises, not one clean-up of one spot. The app still pairs them because it pairs photos by place and time.
* Impact warns that each photo was "submitted long after it was taken". That is true of these photos, and the check is working as designed.
* Impact marks the Hingalganj photos for review because the files on Commons were saved by Adobe Lightroom. That is also true, and it shows the editing-software check at work.
