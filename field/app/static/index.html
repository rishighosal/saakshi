A live view of this device and the system it belongs to. The numbers update every few seconds.

1 · Capture and remember

A photo becomes a point in the device's own Qdrant Edge shard: a 512-d CLIP vector for the picture, a BM25 sparse vector for the note, and a payload with GPS, time, site and status. Faces are found and the sync policy decides, on the device, what may leave it.

2 · Search without a network

Every search runs in process on the device: dense + keyword search fused with RRF, recency and distance re-scoring with Qdrant formulas, geo and payload filters, MMR for variety. The HNSW index is built in the background when the device is idle.

3 · Sync what matters first

Vectors travel first (a few KB), photos after, most urgent first. On a slow link routine photos wait or go compressed. Repeat shots are linked, not re-uploaded.

4 · Share by region

The server keeps one shard per project region. A device mirrors only its regions: a snapshot the first time, then only what changed. Cloud results (Cloudinary AI checks) flow back the same way.
